## Header

**Where Worlds Go to Fall**
*The Nether gives power. The End gives reach.*

End dimension mod concept for Minecraft Java 26.3+ · Concept art is inspirational only, not final in-game visuals · Purple marks the endgame.

## 1 · The setting: four layers of void  *(design doc §2, §3, §5.1)*

**Picture — the four layers (`01_layers.jpg`).** A pale, bone-colored island floats in a dark violet sky with tall lilac chorus stalks and a small ruined purple tower on top. Long teal crystals hang beneath it. Lower down, a thin glowing teal line crosses the whole image — the tide — with two manta-like Skates gliding just above it, their wing spots glowing. At the very bottom, a jagged crack of hot magenta light: the Fracture.

**Layer cards (each points to its part of the picture):**
- **The Rim** — islands on top, close to vanilla. Home of the four surface biomes.
- **The Underside** — upside-down biomes hanging under big islands. Crystals, roots, low gravity.
- **The Drift** — open void between islands. Debris fields and Void Tides.
- **The Fracture** — the deepest layer. The final boss arena.

**Picture — Void Tides (`07_void_tides.jpg`).** Islands float above a wide glowing teal surface; a dashed line above it shows where the tide was a moment ago; muted, drowned islands are visible below the surface.
*Caption:* Void Tides — the deadly layer rises and falls on a timer, exposing hidden islands (and their loot) for a while.

**Table — what existing End mods already own:**

| Existing End mod | What it already owns |
|---|---|
| Nullscape | Vast, desolate vertical terrain (384 tall) |
| BetterEnd | Lush alien biomes, nebula sky |
| Enderscape | Polished vanilla+ overhaul, great sound; already on 26.1.2 |
| Endergetic Expansion | Poise Forest, nicer gateways |
| Unusual End | Flying ships, trader islands |
| End Beyond / End: Expanded | Enderite tier above Netherite |
| Dungeons: Echoing Void | Mojang's own End: Endersent, Vengeful Heart of Ender |

**Gaps nobody fills:** the void as a place you can go · getting around before elytra · rewards that aren't another ore tier.

**Vanilla context:** no End update announced (Nov 2026). The Sift arrives in 2027: vibrant, livable, full of souls. So keep this End cold, cosmic and hostile.

**Lore:** the End is the wreckage of a civilization that tried to escape the void and failed. End Cities are its last outposts, Endermen are its descendants (still carrying blocks out of habit), and the dragon is the warden that held the void back.

## 3 · Creatures  *(design doc §8)*

- **Void Skate** (`08_void_skate.jpg` — a large indigo manta-like glider with rows of teal glowing spots, a glowing edge line on each wing, a whip tail and a leather saddle on its back) — a manta-like glider of the Drift. Tame and saddle it to get around *before* elytra. A real progression step.
- **Chorus Grazer** (close-up from the Chorus Reach picture) — a passive food mob.
- **Underside Lurker** (close-up from the Underside picture: a dark teardrop body hanging on a thread, a ring of small pink eyes, long thin legs) — unseen until you're close.
- **Ender castes** *(no picture)* — Hoarders guard the Hoard. Spire Wardens teleport-slam. Echoes copy the last block you placed.
- **Wild Shulkers** *(no picture)* — live on the outer islands and molt, so shells can be farmed without a duplication farm.

## 4 · Progression arc  *(design doc §12)*

1. **Kill the dragon** — the main island cracks open a way down to the Underside.
   ↓
2. **Tame a Void Skate** — explore the outer islands without elytra.
   ↓
3. **Reach the Fallen Spires** — elytra, plus Spire Keys that *rebuild* parts of ruined cities.
   ↓
4. **Descend into the Fracture** — face The Unmade. *(purple: endgame)*

## 5 · Rewards: reach, not raw stats  *(design doc §9, §10)*

**Picture — the items (`11_items.jpg`).** Five items, each on a small pedestal against a dark starry background:

| Item | Description on the board | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| **Void Anchor** | Die in the void and your items drift in a capsule you can recover | A grey-capped glass lantern with a coiling teal strand inside and a chain loop on top |
| **Rift Tether** | A pearl-powered grappling hook | A coil of dark cord, a dashed teal line, and a sea-green pearl in a three-pronged claw |
| **Gateway Compass** | Finds and links End Gateways | A round dark dial with a violet rim, a pink star needle and a tiny glowing gateway diamond |
| **Shulker Satchel** | Wearable storage *(refined in the design doc §9.5: carried as an item, so it never takes the elytra's chest slot)* | A violet bag with a shell-plate flap, a brass clasp and a leather strap |
| **Elytra upgrades** | Boost · brake · hover, via smithing | A pair of grey membrane wings with two small teal-glowing thrusters |

**Picture — Dragon Egg hatching (`09_dragon_egg.jpg`).** A dark egg with glowing pink cracks sits on a stepped altar with four glowing posts, light rays pouring out; a dashed arrow leads to a second altar where a young slate-blue Wyrm with violet wings and pink eyes stands.
*Card:* **Hatch the Dragon Egg** — a long ritual turns the trophy into a companion that grows up. The most-requested idea nobody has done really well.

**No Enderite.** The Nether already owns raw power; the End owns mobility, storage and control of space.

## 6 · Final boss: The Unmade  *(design doc §13)*

**Picture (`10_the_unmade.jpg`).** A blinding white-pink core hangs over a ring of broken, ash-grey island fragments in crimson darkness. Rings of black, magenta-edged shards orbit the core, and dark tendrils reach down from it to the islands. Debris drifts everywhere.

*Card (purple: endgame):* **The Unmade** — the old civilization's heart, half-dissolved into the void. Its island arena falls apart mid-fight, so you juggle movement, Skate riding and elytra. Drop: the power to *stabilize*, placing permanent gravity anchors to build in the Drift.

## 7 · Building it (for the later code session)  *(design doc §20, §22, §23)*

- **Platform** — since 26.1 the game ships unobfuscated (Mojang's own names) and needs Java 25. NeoForge is the stable long-term target, Fabric ports fast. A multiloader setup can do both.
- **Data first** — worldgen JSON, tags and loot tables as data, so quarterly drops are cheap to port. Java only for gravity/void, mobs and the boss. 26.3's `stepped_column_cluster`, `block_transformer` and new transparency rendering all help.
- **Play nice** — add biomes through `#minecraft:is_end` instead of replacing terrain, so it stacks with Nullscape. Add to vanilla loot tables rather than replacing them, as Enderscape does.
- **v0.1 scope** — Chorus Reach + Void Skate + Dragon Egg hatching + Void Anchor. Small, and it already proves the "reach" idea.

**Open questions (yellow sticky notes):**
1. Mood: desolate and eerie, or some lushness?
2. Solo or team? How comfortable with Java?
3. Fabric, NeoForge, or both?
4. Rework the vanilla dragon fight, or build after it?
